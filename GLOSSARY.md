# DERBYO glossary

The main game terms, one wording per language. The PWA, the Flutter app and the server's texts use these and nothing else.
Owner decisions are dated. Kazakh and Ukrainian come later, after a native reader.

## Rules for every text

- No «ты» in rule and result windows: «Готовность не подтверждена…», not «Ты не подтвердил…» (owner, 2026-11-06).
- Losses: red window title. Warnings: gold title. One window at a time.
- One verb per idea: «засчитан(а) сопернику» for any result given to the opponent.
- Leave room for longer languages: Kazakh and Ukrainian run about 30% longer than Russian; nothing is sized to a Russian word.

## Player status (under the name, icon first)

| Meaning | ru | en |
|---|---|---|
| In the match, on screen | ● в игре | ● Online |
| App minimised | ◒ свернул | ◒ Away |
| Connection lost | (broken link) нет связи | (broken link) Offline |
| Two missed turns, app open | 💤 спит | 💤 Idle |
| Pause or «Готов» left unanswered | ⏱ не отвечает | ⏱ Not responding |
| Left the match | ○ покинул матч | ○ Left |

Between rounds an opponent who closed or minimised the app shows «свернул» / «нет связи», not «не отвечает».
Under «Выйти без штрафа» (bottom bar and match menu): «{имя} бездействует / свернул / — нет связи / не отвечает».
